How the rankings work

How the ratings are calculated, and what a projected result means.

The calculation

MIAA football ratings combine a team's average scoring margin with its opponents' ratings:

Power rating=Avg capped margin+Avg opponent rating
Average margin
Each result counts at no more than a 14-point win or 14-point loss. A 35–7 win is treated as +14; a 14–21 loss is −7.
Opponent rating
The average rating of the teams played. Opponent strength is calculated from the connected statewide schedule, including games against teams from other divisions.
Power rating
The two values combined. A higher rating means a stronger set of results after adjusting for schedule strength.
Change
The difference from that team's October 27 baseline after your entered scores are included.

Forfeits

A forfeit counts in the team's win/loss record and capped average margin. For opponent-strength calculations, MIAA instead uses a separate forfeit-adjusted rating that excludes the forfeited game's margin. The standings rank the team using its regular rating, not its forfeit-adjusted rating. This distinction is applied in this calculator.

What the calculator uses

The starting score data is MIAA's October 27, 2026 statewide Arbiter report: 905 completed games, six forfeits, and three matchups without scores. The model includes 262 Massachusetts schools in Divisions 1–8, along with out-of-state opponents appearing on the schedule.

Division 8 starts from MIAA's published October 27 ratings. Divisions 1–7 start from this calculator's statewide model, adjusted to a common baseline. Scores entered under Week 8 are hypothetical until MIAA publishes new results. Every entered score updates records and ratings throughout the state.

Accuracy and playoff rankings

This is an independent projection, not MIAA's official calculator. The published rating formula is known, but MIAA's precise iteration implementation has not been reproduced to the final decimal. This model uses an iterative estimate with a four-decimal stopping threshold and a maximum of 1,000 iterations. Very close teams may change order in official releases.

The top 16 positions in each division indicate the usual tournament ranking cutoff, not a guaranteed playoff berth. Teams also generally need three wins and seven eligible games; forfeits may affect eligibility. Official bracket decisions and other exceptions are not determined by this page.

Using scenarios

Pick a division to see its standings and Week 8 matchups side by side on desktop, or stacked on phones. Enter both scores on a matchup card to recalculate immediately. MAX sets the selected team to win 14–0, which gives the maximum positive football margin for rating purposes. Predict Week 8 fills unentered games across all divisions with deterministic estimates based on October 27 team ratings, points scored, and points allowed. These are automated statistical estimates, not predictions from an external AI service, and are not guaranteed outcomes. Predictions weigh each team's rating most, then its points scored and allowed, then how both teams did against common opponents. Existing manually entered scores are preserved. Clear predictions removes only the estimates still marked as predictions.

Brackets seed the top 16 teams in each division by rating, 1 against 16, 8 against 9, and so on. Later rounds show the higher seed advancing. A warning mark means the team has fewer than 3 wins or 7 games so far.

Select a team's name or open Teams to view all its games and opponents. Results you enter are saved in this browser and can be cleared under Entered; they do not sync between devices.
